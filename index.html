``` ### Sample Pack Workflow Once you upload your audio files, the app works as a complete production pipeline. Here's how it flows: - **Upload & Analysis**: Drag and drop WAV, AIFF, FLAC, or MP3 files (or entire folders). The app automatically analyzes each file to estimate duration, loudness, peak level, and whether it's a loop or one-shot. It also guesses the category, BPM, and key based on filename keywords. - **Organization & Renaming**: Files are sorted into categories like Drums, Loops, Bass, or FX. You can manually override any category using the dropdown in the file table. The batch rename tool lets you apply a professional naming convention (e.g., `Artist_Pack_Category_Key_Mood_BPM_001`) with customizable artist name, pack name, mood, and numbering. - **Product Copy Generation**: Choose from 10 preset product styles (e.g., "Psychological Horror Toolkit") or generate custom copy based on your uploaded files. The app creates a title, short/long description, tags, license terms, and credits — all written in a polished, marketable tone. - **ZIP Export**: Preview the organized folder structure before export. The final ZIP includes audio files sorted by category, plus `README.txt`, `LICENSE.txt`, `CREDITS.txt`, `product-description.txt`, and `file-list.txt`. Perfect for selling on Gumroad, Ko-fi, or Itch.
